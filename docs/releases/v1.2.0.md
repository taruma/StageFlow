## Overview

**StageFlow v1.2.0** expands 3D entity choreography, directorial look-at targeting, continuous camera rig continuity, and production export workflows—while maintaining 100% backward compatibility across existing `v1.0.0` and `v1.1.x` YAML choreography scores.

---

## What's New in v1.2.0

### 🧭 3D Entity Orientation (`pitch` & `roll`) & Vertical Rotation Pivot (`pivot`) ([#18](https://github.com/taruma/stageflow/issues/18), [#20](https://github.com/taruma/stageflow/issues/20))
- **Per-Entity `pitch` & `roll`**: Author X-axis pitch and Z-axis bank across `entities[]`, `blocking:` (Formats A & B), and `shots[].entities` in intrinsic `YXZ` order (`yaw → pitch → roll`), with shortest-arc easing, `cut: true` instant snapping, and independence from yaw-only `look_at`.
- **Configurable Vertical Pivot (`pivot` / `pivot_ratio` / `pivotRatio`)**: Tilt around `"base"` (`0.0`, floor-pinned leans/prone), `"center"` (`0.5`, mid-air flips/banking), `"top"` (`1.0`), or any clamped `0..1` ratio, with $C^0$ forward chaining across tilted cues (`1 - cos(startPitchRad) * cos(startRollRad) < 1e-8` upright lock) and shear-free 60fps 3D transforms with flat footprints and kind-aware rotated label badges.

### 🎯 Dot-Notation Entity Target Sub-Anchors (`<entityKey>.<part>`) ([#19](https://github.com/taruma/stageflow/issues/19))
- Target specific body regions (`.head`/`.eyes`/`.eye`, `.shoulder`/`.chest`, `.shoulder_l` `+0.40w`, `.shoulder_r` `-0.40w`, `.waist`/`.center`, `.feet`/`.base`, `.top`) across `target`, `from`, `focus`, `focus_start`/`focus_end`, multi-entity centroid arrays, `waypoints`, baseline `stage` cameras, and order-independent Jacobi-relaxed `look_at` (with exact-first disambiguation protecting dotted IDs).

### 🗂️ Storyboard Contact Sheet Generator, Viewer & Exports ([#28](https://github.com/taruma/stageflow/issues/28) / PR [#29](https://github.com/taruma/stageflow/pull/29))
- **Studio Storyboard Modal (`File ▾ → Storyboard...`)**: Generates progressive `540p` WebP shot boards across `Stage Cam` and `Observer` views with `Mid`, `Start`, `End`, `Start & End`, and `Start·Mid·End` sampling modes, DP/AC technical metadata cards, framing guides (`Off` / `Thirds` / `Safe` / `All`), and a `? Spec Guide` popover.
- **Single-Frame & Multi-Page Exports**: Export individual frames (`720p`/`1080p`, `PNG`/`WebP`/`JPG`, `Clean Frame` vs `Burn-in`), paginated white-paper **`Contact Sheet`** (`2400px` multi-page `.zip` or `1-Page PNG` override), or **`All Frames (.zip)`** via a zero-dependency STORE ZIP builder (`zipBuilder.ts`).

### 📸 1-Click 1080p Clean Stage Cam PNG Snapshot
- Desktop bottom-left Camera button in `DirectorialTransportBar` mounts `CleanStageCamExportCanvas` offscreen for a single-frame `1080p` PNG capture inheriting active Diorama Theme and `[Blur]` DoF state (`<slug>[_<shot-id>]_<scene-sec>s_snap_<YYYYMMDD_HHmmss_SSS>_1080p.png`).

### 📖 Chronological YAML DSL Release History
- Added **`§8. YAML DSL Release History`** at the bottom of `docs/YAML_AUTHORING_GUIDE.md` (with a `Releases` shortcut pill in `?mode=guide`) tracing YAML authoring additions chronologically from `v0.1.0` through `v1.2.0`.

## Bug Fixes & Parser Parity

- **Continuous Camera Rig `E_prev` Eye Inheritance**: Continuous `track`, 1-point `spline_track`, and `tripod` camera rigs omitting `rig.from`/`rig.position` now inherit the previous shot's terminal eye position $\vec{E}_{\text{prev}}$ seamlessly even across changing `target:` anchors.
- **Parser Alias Parity**: Added `stage.gridStep` camelCase alias, flat root `optics` aliases (`blur`, `depth_of_field`/`depthOfField`, `f_stop`/`fStop`), and flat shot-root optical overrides (`f_stop_start`, `dof_start`, `f_stop_end`, `dof_end`).
- **Stage Cam DoF `deep` Color Consistency**: Routed `dof: 'deep'` through the FBO 1-tap passthrough in `StageCamDofEffect.tsx` when `[Blur]` is ON, eliminating color-management shifts when racking or switching between `shallow` and `deep`.
